Quick help

How do I create a scoreboard?

Choose “Create a scoreboard”, enter a match name, and add between two and eight player names.

How do I add or subtract points?

Use the quick point buttons on a player's card, or choose Custom to enter a positive or negative number. Tap “Save round” to commit the pending changes.

Can I undo a round?

Use the Undo button in the scoreboard to reverse the most recently saved round.

Where is my scoreboard data stored?

The current version saves scoreboard information locally on your device. It does not sync scoreboards to a cloud account.
