PUNTO — SCORE KEEPER

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PUNTO (“the App”) handles information when you use the PUNTO Score Keeper mobile application.

1. Information You Enter

PUNTO lets you create scoreboards, enter player names, record scores, and view round history. This information is stored locally on your device using on-device storage.

The App does not require you to create an account, and the current version does not send your scoreboard information to us or to a remote server.

2. Data Collection and Sharing

The current version of PUNTO does not operate a backend service and does not collect or transmit your scoreboard data. It does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or cloud synchronization.

We do not sell, rent, or share information entered into your scoreboards because that information remains on your device under the current app design.

3. Local Storage and Your Choices

Your scoreboards, player names, round records, and theme preference are saved locally on your device. You can delete a scoreboard from within the App. Removing the App or clearing its app data may also remove locally stored information. Please keep your own records if you need a separate backup.

4. Permissions

PUNTO's core scorekeeping features do not require access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos.

5. Children's Privacy

PUNTO does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not include an account system or a mechanism for sending player names to us. A device user should follow the age and consent requirements that apply in their location.

6. Security

Because score data is stored locally, the security of the device and its operating-system account settings helps protect that information. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so use your device's normal security protections.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features or data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. If a future version introduces features that collect or transmit information, this policy will be updated to explain those practices before or alongside their release.
